# Lyne: One-Page Investment Decision

**Prepared:** 31 July 2026  
**Planning rate:** US$1 = J$160  
**Decision:** **YES — Lyne is conditionally feasible, but only with staged spending and a paid private pilot before a large build-out.**

## What already exists

Lyne is a substantial working product, not only an idea. The repository contains the customer app, staff/management screens, queue service, database, security foundations, walk-in intake, analytics and forecasting. Remaining work is production finishing, web joining/readiness, real-device testing, secure hosting, independent assurance and release.

## What it costs

| Launch route | Jamaican dollars | US dollars | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Founder-led cash floor | **J$2.44M** | **US$15.26k** | Founder completes remaining coding; specialists test security, privacy, deployment and release. |
| Recommended responsible launch | **J$5.66M** | **US$35.38k** | Includes paid product finishing, full testing, security/privacy assurance, first-year hosting, stores/signing and a 15% reserve. |
| Earlier broad ceiling | J$11.0M | US$68.75k | Too broad for the app-only first launch; included outsourcing, sales, larger infrastructure and enterprise allowance. |

First-customer hosting is about **J$182,784 per year**. Apple, Windows signing and data registration take the steady platform/vendor total to approximately **J$338,000 per year**, before support labour.

The prior **J$250,000 sales/travel figure was a full-year allowance**, not one demo. It is removed. A separate **J$100,000** can cover six to eight local meetings and two observations.

## What customers can reasonably pay

- Founding one-branch pilot: quote **J$1.5M**, aim to close near **J$1.2M**, normal floor **J$900,000**.
- One branch after proof: **J$900,000–J$1.4M per year**.
- Two to five branches after proof: **J$1.8M–J$3.2M per year**.
- Five-branch initial rollout with setup/training: roughly **J$4M–J$7M**.

A J$1.2M pilot is about 0.34% of FHC’s reported J$354.34M surplus. International competitors confirm this is a paid software category; Lyne’s price is plausible but must reflect its unproven status.

## When it breaks even

Estimated company cost is **J$1.1M per year excluding founder pay**, or **J$3.5M including a J$200,000 monthly founder salary**. That can be covered by:

- four one-branch annual customers;
- four properly priced pilots; or
- one five-branch initial rollout.

The J$5.66M launch likely has a **three-to-five-year payback** in a realistic case; one early multi-branch contract shortens it.

## Who to target

1. **FHC Credit Union:** primary; confirm queue pain and incumbent status first.
2. **Access Financial:** fast path if the warm route reaches Operations and its app does not already solve the problem.
3. **NCU Student Finance:** first university follow-on.
4. **UWI Mona/diagnostics:** after a measured case study.
5. **Traffic Court/other courts:** discovery only until scheduled sessions work and the IECMS/TIMS boundary is known. No Transport or Traffic Authority is included.

## The go/no-go rule

Spend no more than **J$350,000** on registration, accounts, quotes and qualification. Release the next **J$1.2M** only after a paid pilot, budget-backed letter of intent or grant identifies one branch, three services, a sponsor, baseline and no blocking incumbent.

Continue if 90 days of outreach produces two sponsors and one measurable branch opportunity. Stop or pivot by about 180 days if nobody will sign at **J$900,000 or more**, suitable systems already exist or required integrations make the deal unprofitable.

> **Final answer:** Continue Lyne because the expensive core already exists and institutional pricing can support it. Do not spend J$11M upfront. Stage J$2.44M–J$5.66M, win and measure one private branch, then fund expansion from evidence.
