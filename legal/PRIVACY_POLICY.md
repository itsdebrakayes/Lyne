## 2. What this app does

Q Me Now lets you see how busy a public or private service branch is, join a queue remotely, hold your place, and be told when to set off and when you are called. Businesses and agencies use a separate administrator application to run their counters.

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 You give us

| Data | Why | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Identifies you to staff at the counter | Yes |
| Email address | Sign-in, receipts, service messages | Yes |
| Password | Sign-in (held by our authentication provider; we never see it) | Yes |
| Phone number | Queue updates where push is unavailable | Optional |
| **TRN, National ID, passport details** | So an agency can verify you faster at the counter | **Optional** |
| Photos of identity documents | Same, and to pre-fill the above | **Optional** |
| Payment card details | Premium subscription. Handled by our payment provider; **card numbers never reach our servers** | Only for Premium |

### 3.2 Generated by using the service

- Tickets you take: number, verification code, position, times joined/called/served
- Visit history: which branch, which service, when
- Agencies you save
- Ratings and feedback you submit
- Notification preferences and device push tokens

### 3.3 Collected automatically

- **Approximate location**, only while the app is open and only if you allow it, to estimate travel time and show nearby branches. You can refuse, and the app still works.
- Device and app information: model, OS version, app version, language
- Crash and error diagnostics
- Log data: IP address, timestamps, endpoints called

### 3.4 Sensitive personal data

TRN, National ID and passport numbers are **sensitive personal data** under the DPA. We:

- collect them **only if you choose** to add them — every queue feature works without,
- use them **only** to identify you to the agency serving you,
- never sell, rent or share them for marketing, ever,
- let you delete them individually, without deleting your account.

### 3.5 Children

Q Me Now is not intended for under-18s and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 5. Automated processing

We use statistical models to forecast waits and suggest quieter times. These predictions inform *you*; they do not make decisions about you, do not affect your place in any queue, and produce no legal or similarly significant effect. There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of the DPA.

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPA you have the right to:

- **Be informed** — this policy
- **Access** a copy of your data
- **Rectify** anything inaccurate — most of it is editable in the app
- **Erase** your data — Profile → Delete my account
- **Restrict** or **object to** processing, including a right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Portability** — a machine-readable copy
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting what came before
- **Complain** to the **Office of the Information Commissioner** — you can do that without contacting us first

To exercise any of these: **[PRIVACY EMAIL]**. We respond within **30 days**.

### Deleting your account

Profile → **Delete my account**. This permanently erases your profile, saved agencies, visit history, notifications, saved cards, and **every identity document and scan we hold**, and gives up any queue you are in.

Agencies keep an anonymised record that a visit occurred, for their own service statistics. It contains nothing that identifies you and is no longer personal data.

## 10. How we protect it

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
- Passwords never stored by us; handled by our authentication provider
- Card numbers never touch our servers
- Access controlled by role and by organisation — agency staff can only reach their own organisation's data, enforced server-side on every request
- Optional Face ID / passcode lock on your saved documents
- Identifiers and codes stripped from diagnostic reports before they leave your device
- Audit logging of administrative access

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a breach affecting your rights we will notify the Office of the Information Commissioner and you, as the DPA requires.
